Once you have reviewed your changes, decide what to do with them.

**To commit (keep your changes):**

Type a short message in the box at the top of the **Source Control** panel (e.g. `Add header styles`), then press `Ctrl+Enter` or click the checkmark **(v)** button.
SnapTrack will ask you to confirm, then save a commit to your history.

> **Tip:** Leave the message empty and SnapTrack will suggest one for you, like *"Update index.php (+2 lines)"*.

**To discard (undo your changes):**

Click the **Discard** icon next to a single file -- or click **Discard All** in the **...** menu to undo everything.
A safety checkpoint is saved automatically before every discard, so nothing is ever permanently lost.
