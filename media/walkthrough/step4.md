Every commit you make is saved in **SnapTrack History**, visible in the Source Control panel.

**What you can do in History:**

- **Expand** any entry to see the files it changed, then click a file for the diff.
- **Right-click** an entry to compare it with your current files, restore your whole project to that point, export a `.patch` or `.zip`, or **pin** it so Clean Up never removes it.
- **Search** the history by message, file name or date.
- **Recently deleted** at the top shows files that exist in history but have been removed from disk -- restore them in one click.

[Open SnapTrack History](command:snaptrack.history)

> You are all set! Keep committing as you work and SnapTrack will build up a full timeline of your project -- without Git, without setup, and without risk.
