Make any change to a file in your project -- edit a line, add a new file, or delete one.

SnapTrack will show it instantly in **Source Control** with a badge:

- **M** -- modified
- **A** -- added
- **D** -- deleted

**To open a side-by-side diff:**

Click the file name in the **Changes** list (or click the diff icon on the right). You will see the last committed version on the left and your current version on the right.

> **Tip:** You can also right-click any line in the editor and choose **Revert Change at Cursor** to undo just that block.
